How to play Ophelia

  • Ace 1, number cards face value, Jack 11, Queen 12, King 13.
  • A new card turns over by itself. A King is 13 alone: tap it to send it to the pile.
  • If your card makes 13 with, or matches, the top or bottom card of the line, tap both to clear them.
  • If it fits nowhere, tap Lay down. It goes at the bottom of the line.
  • The top and bottom cards together, or the two cards side by side at either end, also clear when they make 13 or a pair.
  • You may also add up three or more cards to make 13: your card and cards taken off the top and bottom ends of the line, with no gaps. Tap them one at a time. It's allowed, but it leaves other cards without partners, so use it with care.
  • Tap the stack in the middle to see every card in the line.
  • The phone buzzes and the cards glow when a match is waiting.
  • Win by emptying the line once the deck runs out.
